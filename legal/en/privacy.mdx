## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Opal x Terminus ("we", "us") collects through the website at https://tenacity.rip, the Tenacity client and launcher, and the account, licensing, chat, cosmetics, and support systems behind them, and how that information is used and shared. We collect as little as the services need to work, and this policy describes what the services actually do.

## 2. Information you provide

- **Account details:** an email address and a password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; we cannot read them. If you enable two-factor authentication or add a passkey, we store what is needed to verify it. Passkeys never leave your device; we keep only the public key.
- **Sign-in codes:** if you sign in with an email code, we send a short-lived, single-use code to your address.
- **Support messages:** what you write to us, kept so we can resolve your issue.

## 3. Purchases

Tenacity is not sold on its own; it comes with an Opal or Terminus license bought at https://opal.wtf. That purchase, and the payment data that goes with it, is covered by the Opal Privacy Policy at https://opal.wtf/privacy. tenacity.rip itself takes no payments and never sees card details. We do read which licenses your account holds, so we can tell whether you have Tenacity access and where it came from.

## 4. Device and client data

- **License enforcement:** the launcher and client read a device identifier when you sign in, so a license cannot be shared.
- **Sign-in sessions:** we keep a record of the sessions signed in to your account so you can see and end them from your dashboard.
- **Minecraft accounts:** when you use Tenacity in game, the Minecraft account you play on (its UUID and name) is linked to your Tenacity account, so we can show your cosmetics and badges and enforce bans.
- **Diagnostics:** the client may report technical events, such as launch errors, so we can find and fix problems.

## 5. Chat, cosmetics, and scripts

- Tenacity includes a chat shared with other clients on the same network. Messages you send are relayed to other users and kept for a limited time for moderation and abuse handling. Messages pass through automated moderation.
- Cosmetics and badges you equip are visible to other users of clients on that network, together with your Minecraft name.
- Scripts run on your own computer. We do not collect the scripts you run.

## 6. Analytics

tenacity.rip uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

## 7. Cookies

- **tn_session:** keeps you signed in. HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read it.
- **tn_jwt:** a short-lived token that authenticates your dashboard requests. Also HttpOnly.
- **tn_passkey_challenge:** a temporary value used while you add or use a passkey. It expires after five minutes.
- **tn_locale:** remembers the language you are reading the site in.

That is the whole list. We set no advertising or tracking cookies. The media section of the home page embeds YouTube videos, and YouTube may set its own cookies when you play one.

## 8. How we use information

- To provide the services: signing you in, checking your access, delivering downloads, and running chat and cosmetics.
- To enforce licensing and prevent abuse, including device checks and bans.
- To moderate chat.
- To fix problems using diagnostics.
- To send transactional email, such as sign-in codes, verification links, and security notices. We do not send marketing email.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 9. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate Tenacity, such as our email delivery provider and our hosting infrastructure, and with the developers who operate the services. Ban and moderation records are shared with the other clients on the same network so that a ban applies across it. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to investigate fraud or protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, our developers, or others. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

## 10. Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Chat and moderation logs are kept for limited periods appropriate to abuse handling. If you want your account deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove what we are not legally required to keep.

## 11. Security

We protect your information with salted password hashing, passkeys, HttpOnly authentication cookies, transport encryption, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 12. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your personal information by contacting us at the address below. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, see https://opal.wtf/gdpr for your rights and the legal bases we rely on. If you are a California resident, see https://opal.wtf/ccpa for your rights under the CCPA. Those pages apply to Tenacity as well.

## 13. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you are under 13, do not use Tenacity or send us personal information. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above always reflects the current version. For significant changes we will make reasonable efforts to give notice, for example on the dashboard or by email.

## 15. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to exercise your rights, go to support@tenacity.rip. We will respond as quickly as we can, and within any deadline the law sets.
